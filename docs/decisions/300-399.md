### 300. A card's command in a shape its translation doesn't read is unknown, never left out — active (2026-11-25)
A command step of a card's play that no case of the translation takes (`SimAdapter.Translate`) is an unknown with its reason where it runs, under its condition (`SimAdapter.Untranslated`): a named gap's reason (CommandsNotTranslated), the shape's reason for a translated command in a shape its case doesn't read (`SimAdapter.ShapesNotFollowed`: a creature other than your Osty killed, other than the target stunned, its HP set or its block taken, an arrow pulled out of another, stars for another player, a power off anyone but the card's owner), or "a use of it the simulator doesn't follow" for anything else (a power's own command its model can't run, a command a game update added). Only a command reviewed as changing nothing the simulator predicts (CommandsOutsideTheFight) is nothing. The shape checks are one pure function of the step's code (`ShapeNotFollowed`), which the card scan asks of every card on both branches (`StepTranslationTests`: a new card in another shape fails the release; End of Days' Doom kill, read into its play, is the one known and tracked). The same goes inside the translations: damage, block, a power the simulator reads, energy, a heal or a summon to a creature the translation can't name, an attack whose targeting it can't read, an orb it can't name and a make it can't read are unknown with why. Two shapes found this way are translated instead: a power taken off the card's owner (`RemoveOwnPowerAction`: Rain Shot's Aim and Quietus after its shot, its AfterRemoved told), and the hittable opponents of the card's owner as every enemy (`StepValues.IsEveryEnemy`: Timber's damage to every enemy when its first hit kills).
*Why:* the owner's decision of 2026-11-25 (decision #92: not modelled means uncertain): a translated command whose shape check failed fell through to nothing, so its effect was silently missing from the prediction (Timber's on-kill damage and Rain Shot's removals were).
*Replaced:* the fallthrough of decision #292, which made only CommandsNotTranslated unknown and left every other step that no case took out.
*What changed:* Timber's damage to every enemy after a kill and Rain Shot's removal of your Aim and Quietus are predicted; End of Days shows `?` where its play would kill a doomed enemy (left out before). The card snapshots: the plays of Rain Shot (5 lines: two removals after the shot's hit) and Timber (5 lines: its on-kill attack to every enemy under its kill condition) gain their actions; no outcome changes (no kill or Aim on their boards). The single-player golden is unchanged.

### 301. The simulator stops at the fight's end — active (2026-11-25)
Once a branch's fight is over (it has enemies, and FightEnding holds: no primary enemy alive, no listener's ShouldStopCombatFromEnding holding it back, as CombatManager.IsCombatEnding asks), the simulator does there what the game does: every later action of the play is set aside untouched (each game command returns at its start once CombatManager.IsEnding or IsOverOrEnding: `SimEngine.WhileFighting`), a combat hook it runs as a step reaches no one (Hook.IterateCombatHookListeners: CombatManager.ShouldSkipCombatEffect), but for the dispatches the game makes to every listener whatever the fight's state (`SimEngine.HooksAfterTheFightEnds`: AfterCardPlayed, and the death sequence's hooks, which run inside the damage), a read model's command step is a no-op though its listener still flashes (`ReadModel.StoppedOnceTheFightEnds`), what the killing hit raised (an arrow coming loose, a power's listeners, a card made) reaches no one, a card's later plays don't happen (OnPlayWrapper's loop breaks), its enchantment's OnPlay does nothing, and no end of turn or enemy turn follows (one that ends during the turn's end stops there). `FightEndTests` reads both from the game's code on both branches: each hook the simulator stops or runs loops as the game's does, and each command it stops has its guard (or a reviewed reason). The play check holds a play that won the fight to the state it left: at the action's AfterFinished the fight's cleanup hasn't run yet (ActionExecutor runs CheckWinCondition, then EndCombatInternal, after it), so the state after the play, unexplained powers, your cards, relics and resources, the powers a play spends and every tab's fire are checked as for any play; only the history's shortfalls stay unchecked (the game logs no damage from the killing hit on).
*Why:* the owner's decision of 2026-11-25: a play that won the fight was simulated past it, so its end of turn (block, damage to you, procs) and its later effects showed up in predictions and made the play check skip it.
*Replaced:* the turn's end and enemy turn run after a won fight, and every command of a play run whatever the fight's state (SimEngine.FightEnding asked only in the enemy turn); the play check's "the fight ended" skips of the state after the play (decision #298 made Play Preview's a check).
*What changed:* after a play that wins the fight, no end-of-turn block, damage or procs, no energy, draws, cards or block from effects after the killing hit (Sunder's energy, Compost's, Twine's block, Explosive's blast on the last enemy). The single-player golden: 11 of 600 fights change, every one a play that kills the last enemy, every change your block as the enemy turn would start (the end of turn's Plating, Frost and Feel No Pain block, no longer gained). The card snapshots are unchanged (no kill on their boards). The parity records of the read models, 204 lines in 12 files, change only in fights a play or the turn's end won.
