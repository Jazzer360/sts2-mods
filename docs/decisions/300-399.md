### 300. A card's command in a shape its translation doesn't read is unknown, never left out — active (2026-11-25)
A command step of a card's play that no case of the translation takes (`SimAdapter.Translate`) is an unknown with its reason where it runs, under its condition (`SimAdapter.Untranslated`): a named gap's reason (CommandsNotTranslated), the shape's reason for a translated command in a shape its case doesn't read (`SimAdapter.ShapesNotFollowed`: a creature other than your Osty killed, other than the target stunned, its HP set or its block taken, an arrow pulled out of another, stars for another player, a power off anyone but the card's owner), or "a use of it the simulator doesn't follow" for anything else (a power's own command its model can't run, a command a game update added). Only a command reviewed as changing nothing the simulator predicts (CommandsOutsideTheFight) is nothing. The shape checks are one pure function of the step's code (`ShapeNotFollowed`), which the card scan asks of every card on both branches (`StepTranslationTests`: a new card in another shape fails the release; End of Days' Doom kill, read into its play, is the one known and tracked). The same goes inside the translations: damage, block, a power the simulator reads, energy, a heal or a summon to a creature the translation can't name, an attack whose targeting it can't read, an orb it can't name and a make it can't read are unknown with why. Two shapes found this way are translated instead: a power taken off the card's owner (`RemoveOwnPowerAction`: Rain Shot's Aim and Quietus after its shot, its AfterRemoved told), and the hittable opponents of the card's owner as every enemy (`StepValues.IsEveryEnemy`: Timber's damage to every enemy when its first hit kills).
*Why:* the owner's decision of 2026-11-25 (decision #92: not modelled means uncertain): a translated command whose shape check failed fell through to nothing, so its effect was silently missing from the prediction (Timber's on-kill damage and Rain Shot's removals were).
*Replaced:* the fallthrough of decision #292, which made only CommandsNotTranslated unknown and left every other step that no case took out.
*What changed:* Timber's damage to every enemy after a kill and Rain Shot's removal of your Aim and Quietus are predicted; End of Days shows `?` where its play would kill a doomed enemy (left out before). The card snapshots: the plays of Rain Shot (5 lines: two removals after the shot's hit) and Timber (5 lines: its on-kill attack to every enemy under its kill condition) gain their actions; no outcome changes (no kill or Aim on their boards). The single-player golden is unchanged.
