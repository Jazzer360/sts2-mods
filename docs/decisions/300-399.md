### 300. A card's command in a shape its translation doesn't read is unknown, never left out — active (2026-11-25)

### 301. The simulator stops at the fight's end — active (2026-11-25)
Once a branch's fight is over (it has enemies, and FightEnding holds: no primary enemy alive, no listener's ShouldStopCombatFromEnding holding it back, as CombatManager.IsCombatEnding asks), the simulator does there what the game does: every later action of the play is set aside untouched (each game command returns at its start once CombatManager.IsEnding or IsOverOrEnding: `SimEngine.WhileFighting`), a combat hook it runs as a step reaches no one (Hook.IterateCombatHookListeners: CombatManager.ShouldSkipCombatEffect), but for the dispatches the game makes to every listener whatever the fight's state (`SimEngine.HooksAfterTheFightEnds`: AfterCardPlayed, and the death sequence's hooks, which run inside the damage), a read model's command step is a no-op though its listener still flashes (`ReadModel.StoppedOnceTheFightEnds`), what the killing hit raised (an arrow coming loose, a power's listeners, a card made) reaches no one, a card's later plays don't happen (OnPlayWrapper's loop breaks), its enchantment's OnPlay does nothing, and no end of turn or enemy turn follows (one that ends during the turn's end stops there). `FightEndTests` reads both from the game's code on both branches: each hook the simulator stops or runs loops as the game's does, and each command it stops has its guard (or a reviewed reason). *Why:* the owner's decision of 2026-11-25: a play that won the fight was simulated past it, so its end of turn (block, damage to you, procs) and its later effects showed up in predictions and made the play check skip it.
*Replaced:* the turn's end and enemy turn run after a won fight, and every command of a play run whatever the fight's state (SimEngine.FightEnding asked only in the enemy turn); the play check's "the fight ended" skips of the state after the play (decision #298 made Play Preview's a check).

### 302. The enemies' side's turn end is simulated, from the listeners' own code — active (2026-11-25)
After the enemy turn's last move the simulator runs the enemies' side's turn end as CombatManager.EndEnemyTurnInternal does (`SimEngine.EnemyTurnEnd`): Hook.BeforeSideTurnEnd (every listener's VeryEarly, then Early, then its own), then Hook.AfterSideTurnEnd (then Late), in listener order, its side the enemies' and its participants the enemies in the fight, while the fight goes on. PowerCmd.TickDownDuration is read as a tick that a power's SkipNextDurationTick spends instead (`DecrementPowerStep.TicksDuration`, `SimState.TickSkips`, read from the game's powers by the adapter): a debuff new on a creature of the players' side gets it as PowerCmd.Apply lands it, on beta only during the enemies' turn and on main on any turn (`BranchRules.DebuffTickSkippedOnTheEnemiesTurnOnly`, `MainRule.DebuffSkipsTickOnAnyTurn`). *Replaced:* a turn's-end step on the enemies' side read as never running and dropped ("its commands wait on the enemies' side's turn ending, which no simulation reaches"), and the players' turn end run for players' listeners only.
*What changed:* nothing shown: no prediction reads past the enemy turn yet. The single-player golden and the card snapshots are unchanged.
