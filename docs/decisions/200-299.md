### 200. A card made is one event, carrying the card: hooks test its facts — active (2026-11-24)
A card made (CardPileCmd.AddGeneratedCardToCombat and its helpers, a forge, a card turning into another after its play, a transform) is one card event, `CardEvent.Generated`, about the card its code names: its class and its canonical type and tags (`CardEventAction.Card`, `TakeFromHandAction.Made`; `SimState.EventCard` while its listeners run, in every path the event takes, a card joining your hand one at a time included; the fight's log records its type and class). A hook tests the card's facts as its code does (`HookFact.CardIs`: `card is Sadness`; `HookFact.CardType`: `card.Type == CardType.Status`), so any card made works, and a card a game update makes is told as it is. A card made by a roll or a pick has no class to tell: its type is none (not the played card's). The card scan holds every class a play creates to a make or a transform that carries it (`CardEffects.CreatedNotMade`), where it held Sadness alone. `CardEvent` keeps its numbers (a play's script and a report's start state carry them): 4 and 5 are unused.
*Why:* the event kinds `GeneratedStatus` and `GeneratedSadness` were one card's and one type's names in the engine (`ReadModel` tested `named == "Sadness" && Event == GeneratedSadness`), and they were wrong: a Sadness is a Status (`Sadness: base(-1, CardType.Status, ...)`), which Smokestack's and Trash to Treasure's `card.Type == CardType.Status` hear; the predictions left them out for every card that makes one. A drawn card already carried its profile; a made one now carries its card the same way.
*Replaced:* `CardEvent.GeneratedStatus` and `GeneratedSadness`, `MadeCards.Kind` and `KindOf`, `MadeCards.OnBoard`'s Statuses and Sadness counts, the card scan's Sadness check. The card-outcomes snapshot lists a made card as `Generated` (it listed `GeneratedStatus` and `GeneratedSadness` apart; the counts are their sums, nothing else changes); the card-plays snapshot's made-card events carry their card.

### 201. A model's own method is read where it's called; an enemy's poison ticks from PoisonPower's code — active (2026-11-24)
A power's call to a method of its own class (PoisonPower.AfterSideTurnStart's `Trigger()`, Black Hole's `DealDamageToAllEnemies`) is read where it's called, its parameters what the call passes (`ProgramReader`'s own-method rule, which cards already had), where it was a step named by its power and method that no translator knew; another power's (Outbreak's `PoisonPower.Trigger` on each enemy's poison) stays such a step. With `Math.Min`/`Max` (`HookOperation.Min`, `Max`), a power summed over the owner's living opponents (`HookFact.OpponentsPowerAmount`: TriggerCount's Accelerant) and `base.Owner.IsAlive`, PoisonPower reads whole and is modelled from its code: as the enemies' turn starts, in the game's listener order (`SimEngine.EnemyTurnStart`), each enemy's poison ticks TriggerCount times (its stacks at most, 1 + every living opponent's Accelerant read as it ticks), a damage of its stacks to its owner each (Unblockable | Unpowered, no dealer, through the damage math and the HP-loss pipeline), a stack less while it lives. An enemy's poison stacks are its PoisonPower entry (`SimEnemy.PoisonStacks` reads and sets it), so it lands as any power lands and is a listener in its place among the enemy's powers. Hardened Shell's budget resets at its own BeforeSideTurnStart (its code), before any tick. The enemy turn no longer ticks poison: the enemies the turn's start killed skip their moves (`SkippedPoisoned`). What stays the engine's: the game's preview of the next tick (`SimEnemy.Poison`, PoisonPower.CalculateTotalDamageNextTurn as the adapter reads it, which the play check compares), raised as stacks and Accelerant land.
*Why:* the tick was hand-coded in the enemy turn as one hit of the preview's total, after every turn-start listener: with Accelerant the game deals one hit per tick (Slippery and Hard to Kill cap each), and the tick's place is its power's among the listeners. Decision #195: the effect from its code.
*Replaced:* the enemy turn's poison tick and its Hardened Shell reset; the engine's Poison special cases where a power's amount is read (`AmountOf`, the conditions' power counts). The single-player golden and the card snapshots are unchanged (every golden poison ticks once: one hit of its stacks, as before).

### 202. A flash on a test the reader can't translate is an unknown flash, for the play check only — active (2026-11-24)
A power's or relic's `Flash()` whose condition holds a test the reader has no translation for is an unknown flash (`FlashStep.Unknown`, the gap's words), not a gap of the whole hook body: the rest of the body reads as before, each other step on its own condition (a step guarded by the same test is still a gap). A read model logs it under its own key in the flash log (`SimKeys.UnknownFlash`: "?Name"), apart from its known flashes; nothing shown reads it. The play check (`CardTabs.FromLog`, `CardPrediction.FlashUnknown`, `ProcAudit`) treats a source the play reached an unknown flash of as possibly flashing: not expected to flash, not reported for flashing, the skip counted with its reason ("its code flashes on a condition the reader doesn't read").
*Why:* decision #199 made a flash a step, which turned every hook whose flash sat on an untranslatable test into a gap (Chemical X, Shriek, Stratagem, Vital Spark, Mighty Wings, Music Box), hiding what else their bodies need. A flash changes nothing in the fight: only the check that compares flashes needs to know it's unknown (decision #92: unknown, counted, never guessed).
*What changed:* Chemical X's BeforeCardPlayed reads (its unknown flash alone); Shriek, Stratagem, Vital Spark, Mighty Wings and Music Box now name their next gap (hook-gaps.txt). No class became read by the rule; the snapshots of card plays and outcomes and the single-player golden are unchanged.

### 204. A power a read model applies has the applier its code names, is heard by the listeners, and a debuff on a player owner crosses its tab — active (2026-11-24)
PowerCmd.Apply's applier in a read hook is its owner, the applier the power keeps (`base.Applier`: Oblivion's Doom, Tender's Strength and Dexterity) or no one (a null: Lagavulin Matriarch's Dominion), `ApplyPowerStep.By`, where any applier but the owner was a gap; a power that applies as its applier keeps its applier as its Data (`ModelSpec.UsesApplier`). *Replaced:* the adapter's Oblivion case (its applier in Data: the rule for every read power that asks it does it now).

### 207. Every power landing is heard; inside a hit, once the hit is over — active (2026-11-24)
Every power a model applies is told to the AfterPowerApplied listeners (the game's Hook.AfterPowerAmountChanged), hand-written models' too: one in a hook that can branch through `SimEngine.ApplyAndTell` (Vital Spark's Tainted; the engine's own Blossom Shot Stretch), one inside a hit or a death, where hooks run on the state given and can't branch (Hand Drill's AfterBlockBroken, Monarch's Gaze's AfterDamageGiven, Red Skull's AfterDamageReceived, and a read model's AfterDamageGiven, AfterDamageReceived or AfterDeath), through `SimEngine.ApplyInHit`: the power lands at once (what the rest of the hit reads), and its listeners are told once the hit or the action is over (`SimState.PendingPowers`, drained by `DrainPending` with the card events raised there, in the order they landed). The game tells them inside the hit; nothing a listener does today (Sleight of Flesh's damage, Vicious' draw, Shroud's block) changes the rest of the hit it landed in.
*Why:* the hand-written models called `ApplyPower` directly, so Hand Drill's Vulnerable set off neither Sleight of Flesh's damage nor Vicious' draw (HandDrill.AfterBlockBroken: `PowerCmd.Apply<VulnerablePower>(choiceContext, target, ..., base.Owner.Creature, null)`; SleightOfFleshPower and ViciousPower answer AfterPowerAmountChanged with `applier == base.Owner`); and a read model's application inside a hit was told in place, an error if a listener branched there (a draw), the same combination a read Hand Drill would reach. Card events raised inside a hit already wait so (`Raise`).
*Replaced:* `ApplyPower` called by models directly; a read model's in-hit telling in place. An enemy move's powers (`MovePowers`) stay told to no one: the enemy turn runs on one state, and no listener reacts to an enemy's application today (the method list names it).
